Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Here is how we handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, such as your email address during registration and the expense details (amount, category, date) that you log in the application.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the service. We use it to generate your spending summaries, categorize your expenses, and manage your user account.

3. Data Storage

We store your data securely. We implement industry-standard encryption and security protocols to ensure your financial information remains private and protected from unauthorized access.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to outside parties. We only use essential third-party services (like hosting) that are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us via the support email provided in your account settings.
